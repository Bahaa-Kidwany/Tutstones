ANCIENT HERITAGE & MODERN LUXURY

The Beauty of Egyptian Stone:

Egypt is renowned for it’s rich natural stone resources and varieties with elegant colors, unique patterns and exceptional durability.

Galala & Sunny Beige

World-famous Egyptian beige marbles featuring warm sunlit tones, consistent grain structure, and high polishing capacity for grand floorings.

Silvia & Triesta

Exquisite vein-cut and cross-cut Egyptian marbles offering sophisticated horizontal movement for walls, bathrooms, and modern interiors.

Sinai Pearl & Grey

Ultra-durable micro-crystalline grey limestone/marble engineered by geological pressure for heavy traffic plazas and exterior facades.

Imperial Aswan Granite

Historic red, pink, and black Egyptian granites mined near Aswan—renowned since the era of Pharaohs for unyielding strength.
