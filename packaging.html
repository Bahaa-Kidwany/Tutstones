Packaging & Container Loading

Reinforced seaworthy wooden crates, A-frame slab bundles, ISPM-15 fumigation, and safe container shipping worldwide.

ZERO DAMAGE GUARANTEE

Export-Grade Seaworthy Packaging

Exporting natural Egyptian marble and granite across long oceanic routes demands immaculate packaging engineering. At TUT Stones, every order is packed using heat-treated ISPM-15 compliant hardwood timber to ensure total stability against rough sea transit.

Slabs and tiles are separated by protective polyethylene foam sheets, wrapped in waterproof plastic covers, and braced inside heavy duty wooden crates or A-frame bundles.

ISPM-15 Certified

Official heat treatment & fumigation stamp on all wooden crates.

20ft Container Payload

Maximized tonnage capacity with balanced weight distribution.

OUR PACKAGING METHODS

Tailored Protection For Every Product

Seaworthy Wooden Crates

Used for cut-to-size tiles, stairs, and vanity tops. Constructed from thick hardwood planks, lined with styrofoam padding and plastic wrap.

A-Frame Wooden Bundles

For large gangsaw slabs (20mm & 30mm thickness). Slabs are bound back-to-back with polished faces protected by plastic film and steel strapping.

Moisture & Dust Shield

Every crate and bundle is covered with heavy-duty polyethylene shrink wrap to shield against sea moisture, humidity, and dust during vessel transit.

Container Lashing & Bracing

Crates and wooden bundles are anchored to the container floor using heavy-duty steel wire ropes, wooden chocks, and tension belts to eliminate shift during voyage.

Weight Distribution Optimization

Containers are loaded up to allowable port weight limits (typically 27 metric tons for 20ft containers) strictly adhering to destination country port regulations.

Export Documentation

Every container leaves Egyptian ports with complete export documentation: Bill of Lading (B/L), Certificate of Origin (EUR.1 / Form A), Packing List, and Commercial Invoice.
