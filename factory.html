THE PRODUCTION PROCESS

Step-by-Step Factory Workflow

1. Block Selection & Inspection

Raw marble and granite blocks extracted from Sinai, Minya, and Aswan quarries are individually inspected for micro-fissures, structural density, and color uniformity.

2. Primary Gangsaw Cutting

High-speed diamond multi-blade gang saws cut giant raw blocks into slabs of standard thicknesses (20mm, 30mm, or custom dimensions).

3. Resin Treatment & Polishing

Slabs are dried in automated ovens, reinforced with fibreglass backing mesh when required, and polished with multi-head abrasives for high-gloss shine.

4. Surface Finish Execution

We apply versatile surface finishes to customer orders: Polished, Honed, Brushed (Antique), Bush-Hammered, Flamed, and Split-Face (Rock Face).

5. Cut-to-Size & Edge Profiling

Laser-guided CNC bridge cutters trim slabs into precise tile dimensions (60x60, 30x60, 80x80cm, etc.) or custom architectural project components.

6. Final Quality Inspection

Every batch undergoes piece-by-piece inspection for color matching, diagonal squareness, thickness tolerance, and crack-free perfection before packing.
