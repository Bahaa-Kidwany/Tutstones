WHO WE ARE

A Legacy of Pure Egyptian Stone Artistry

Founded in 2000, TUT Stones is a distinguished Egyptian exporter of premium marble and granite, delivering the timeless beauty of natural stone to clients around the world. Inspired by Egypt's rich geological heritage and renowned craftsmanship, we transform exceptional raw materials into refined products that elevate architectural and interior design projects.

At TUT Stones, we understand that natural stone is more than a building material—it is a statement of luxury, character, and lasting value. Our extensive collection of Egyptian marble and granite is designed to meet the highest standards of architects, developers, contractors, wholesalers, and distributors across global markets.

We believe in building long-term partnerships based on trust, transparency, and professionalism. From material selection to packaging and logistics.

THE TUT STONES ADVANTAGE

Why Global Clients Partner With Us

Our commitment to Egyptian natural stone excellence spans raw material quarrying, precision diamond processing, rigorous inspection, and export delivery.

Direct Quarry Sourcing

We select first-choice blocks directly from premier Egyptian marble and granite quarries in Sinai, Suez, Minya, and Aswan to ensure color consistency and structural density.

Custom Cut-To-Size

Equipped with modern CNC bridge cutters and automated polishing lines, we fulfill exact architectural specifications for tiles, gangsaw slabs, and custom profiles.

Full Export Logistics

From seaworthy wooden crates to container weight optimization, moisture insulation, and ISPM-15 fumigation certificates, we manage global shipping smoothly.
